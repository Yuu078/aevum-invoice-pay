PastiPay

The stablecoin invoice that can't be paid to the wrong person — Confirmation of Payee, enforced on-chain.
Aevum Testnet · chain 44005 · settles in tUSD8 wallet: not connected

1 · Create an invoice

You are the payee. Your name is bound to your wallet address on-chain — your client can only pay after confirming it.

2 · Pay an invoice — with Confirmation of Payee

Load an invoice, then confirm the payee's name. The contract itself refuses payment if the name you confirm doesn't match the name on the invoice.
